# The Living Hangeul District — first golden slice

The world is a collectible architectural diorama: curved Korean-inspired tile roofs, visible timber brackets and hanji screens, a ceramic learning dais, a quiet plum garden and miniature contemporary skyline. Korean influence comes from construction and materials, not invented glyphs or borrowed game assets.

The first chosen mascot is the Ceramic Seed Spirit: pear-shaped porcelain/celadon silhouette, asymmetric folded paper and ceramic leaf ornaments, scalloped collar, brass growth inlay and tiny blossom ornaments. This is Codex's documented art/technical selection, not an assertion that the user approved it. See concepts/DESIGN_REVIEW.md for the three alternatives and rubric.

The Blender geometry is the actual model, interpreted from the generated concept rather than claiming pixel-identical production. The original concept sheet is never used as a mesh or proof of 3D delivery. Editable .blend sources, scripts and exported clips are retained.

Shape rules: curved roofs and inlaid ceramic rhythms, short substantial timber supports, geometric paper folds, broad readable character masses and sparse blossom accents. Static architectural geometry shares material primitives; lantern pivots and character parts remain movable. No fake baked Hangul lettering.

See COLOR_MATERIAL_SYSTEM.md and CAMERA_LANGUAGE.md for color, roughness, lighting, camera and reduced-motion rules. Continue this language in later districts, using the same prop/material library. Do not populate six unrelated placeholder environments to mark expansion complete.

Evidence is actual model renders in quality-reports/, three posters in assets/posters/, validated GLBs in exports/v1/ and the asset manifest. Release 0.2.0 is the first portable slice; physical-device frame-rate validation and the remaining districts are still outstanding.
