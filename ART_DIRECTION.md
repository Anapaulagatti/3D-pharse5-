# THE LIVING HANGEUL DISTRICT — Art Direction (Codex studio only)

## Vision

A tiny, handcrafted Korean district where every learner's progress turns loose Hangul-inspired building blocks into a living neighborhood. The world should feel collectible, tactile, bright, welcoming and extremely deliberate in silhouette, not like a generic Roblox/Animal Crossing/Bufopia clone.

## Style Signature

**Hanji Origami + Celadon Ceramic + Miniature Seoul + Gentle Magic**

Four distinct signatures:

1. **Syllable-block architecture**: roofs/tiles/platforms are built from softly rounded jamo-like modules; show Korean writing through correct DOM/SVG only, not fake characters in artwork.
2. **Celadon light**: subtle jade enamel near cyan lantern cores; avoid oversaturated neon everywhere.
3. **Paper craftsmanship**: layered hanji roof shades, origami foliage, miniature folded-paper clouds.
4. **Animated learning pulse**: progress expressed by softly aligning ceramic tiles and turning on warm lanterns, never by arbitrary flashy particles.

## Mascot — design exploration

Produce exactly 3 distinctive options before choosing a final one:

- **Ceramic syllable spirit**: plump asymmetrical porcelain body, removable jamo-like ear stones (not letters painted incorrectly).
- **Hanji seed courier**: folded-paper teardrop with glowing core, believable folds and simple face.
- **Ink lantern familiar**: floating translucent bell form with warm ink droplets and a ceramic rim.

Pick one through a documented rubric (silhouette at 48px, rig simplicity, expression, cultural sensitivity, asset cost, originality). Then produce actual 3D mesh, idle/cheer/ready/shy states, export GLB, and one transparent 2D fallback render.

## Worlds

### 1. HANOK ROOFTOP — signature hero

- Korean-inspired miniature rooftop courtyard, viewable 3/4 isometric and close orbit.
- Sculptural central arena dais (not a stadium).
- Stacked eaves, tiled roof shapes, subtle courtyard stone, plum blossom accent (subtle) and layered Seoul-inspired skyline silhouettes.
- Clear empty space to the right/left for future HTML CTA when integrated.
- One mascot, lanterns, small dust motes. No fireworks by default.

### 2. LIVING LEARNING ALLEY — mastery scene

Six buildings with distinct shapes and art roles:
- Hangul Atelier — interlocking syllable tile workshop.
- Vocabulary Café — warm interior, floating wordless icon sign.
- Listening Metro — miniature subway entry, sound motifs.
- Grammar Library — layered hanji book stack.
- TOPIK Pavilion — focused, quiet testing hall, no loud distractions.
- EPS Workshop — workplace classroom, safety icon motifs (correct safety signage only).

Progress stages represented by 3D lighting/unlock states from demo fixture data in Studio. Never hard-code user mastery into reusable render components.

### 3. CERAMIC LANTERN CELEBRATION — results

One restrained celebration sequence, a kind losing animation, level-up ceramic pulse. Prefer polish over effect count; no full-screen strobe.

## Color

- Ink Navy `#15243A` (base)
- Hanji Cream `#F5EFE1` (warm paper)
- Celadon Jade `#72BEAC` (identity accent)
- Terracotta Coral `#E57B69` (small pop)
- Oxidized Brass `#CFAC67` (reward highlights)
- Sky Haze `#AFC7E0` (background)
- Slate Ink `#293C55` (shadow)

These are **Studio art defaults**, not modifications to Korean Arena current design tokens. Later assets can be retinted with palette metadata during integration.

## Camera and materials

- Stylized orthographic/isometric as default, optional gentle bounded parallax.
- PBR roughness/metalness carefully tuned; no plastic soup.
- One dominant key light, soft fill, gentle AO where affordable.
- Warm/cool contrast; readable volumes at small resolution.
- No giant 4K textures for small props.
- All signs/text correct Korean, preferably rendered in future DOM overlay rather than baked image generation.

## Originality rules

No duplication of named IP, mascot silhouette, UI layout, music or trademark. Korea-inspired architecture is an artistic context, not license to appropriate someone else's commercial visual design.

## Definition of a winning concept

Looks distinctive in: desktop hero screenshot, phone-width frame, black-and-white silhouette, and a flat 2D fallback poster. Feels like a carefully art-directed world, not a random heap of downloaded assets.
