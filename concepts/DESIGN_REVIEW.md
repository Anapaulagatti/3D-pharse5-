Selected A for the first slice, as a technical/art decision by Codex. No user approval is assumed. Preserve B and C as explorations, not rejected assets to delete.

Modeling interpretation: a pear-shaped ceramic body with an asymmetric pair of seed/leaf ornaments; scalloped collar, inset brass growth seam, small feet, expressive mitten hands and warm face. One ornament is cream folded hanji and the other celadon. Avoid animal-copy silhouettes, cultural lettering and borrowed commercial characters. Simplify the fine concept surface pattern into intentional geometry, color variation and original low-resolution paper/stone texture. The model should embody the concept rather than claim pixel-exact reproduction.

Arena interpretation: a floating collectible rooftop courtyard, large curved-eave timber pavilion, small gateway, circular celadon arena dais, plum grove, rock garden, paper lanterns, bridge over a narrow jade water channel, layered abstract Seoul skyline. Preserve open foreground and silhouette. Compose in orthographic 3/4 view; lanterns are warm islands within cool ceramic/roof materials.

Quality bar: inspect actual model renders at desktop and phone widths, clip pose differences, material fidelity and exported model integrity. The concept sheet cannot be used as proof that the real scene has reached this bar.
