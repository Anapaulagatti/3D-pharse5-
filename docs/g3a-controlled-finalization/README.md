## Station canopy/platform resolution candidate

The original 38×299 overlap is a **visual envelope**, not a valid physical collider. Its source texture mixes a roof, landing, sign/pole, railing and garden. One depth root for that entire image confused these ownerships.

The candidate keeps every original source file immutable and preserves all original source pixels outside the bounded local railing detail. It separates:

- Elevated roof assembly: native ownership, root 2398, optional existing-style occlusion fade.
- Canopy support base `(2929,2389,11,9)` and sign-pole base `(2928,2489,11,6)`: independent real physical supports, roots 2398/2495.
- Landing paving: ground ownership beneath Juni.
- Railing: the conflicting end inside the platform is opened; an existing native metal end-post sample terminates it at `(2913,2524,7,6)`, immediately west of the unchanged platform reservation. Retained railing has its own root 2530.
- Garden: original retained foliage/trunk context, root 2649, independent of roof and ground.

The native source detail is confined to local source box `[29,282,74,316]` of the original 74×436 canopy texture. A small 90×36 native paving connector inside the fixed platform reservation joins the opening to the existing platform path. Paving uses clean original station-material reference pixels, with integer translations only; no resizing, sharpening, paid generation or station repainting. [Source masks, footprints and provenance](../../assets-src/gate3/station-platform-resolution/ownership.json).

The station facade retains its previously documented reservation projections of 7px west/78px east/59px south. Neither its logical coordinates nor the historical reservation was rewritten; this retained envelope remains disclosed for final registration review.

The protected platform remains exactly `(2920,2350,100,400)`. Its longitudinal route at x2980, original public approach `(2980,2690)→(2970,2530)`, and local opening approach all pass exact logical+mask checks. Juni's49px silhouette clears the actual support bases; the roof projection is not treated as a wall. Added support guards are outside the protected forecourt patch. Station body collider and accepted forecourt mask remain exact.

### Exact remaining decision — transfer collision ownership

The retained source walk mask has a closed 8px strip at **world x2950–2957 across the public corridor y2626–2754**, corresponding to source rectangle **`(3176,713,8,129)`**. It predates this work. That strip includes real garden/pot context in part, so indiscriminately making the entire strip walkable would be incorrect.

The approved forecourt collision is preserved. Consequently, the fixed platform is traversable from its review spawn, but a complete transfer through the reserved retail/plaza corridor cannot be certified. **Local station-platform production remains BLOCKED.**

The required next decision is either:

1. Authorize a separately bounded collision-ownership reconciliation of the inherited source-edge guard, retaining the actual tree/pot, supports, station collider, entrance, planters and approved paving; or
2. Explicitly approve a pedestrian connector around the retained edge, with its footprint and clearance reviewed before implementation.

Neither exception has been applied. The native roof/railing ownership candidate is submitted for review, not declared full station-platform acceptance.
