Recoverable annotated tag: `checkpoint/gate25-final-approved-20261022`. Earlier approval-pending labels in frozen source/package are historical. No approved file has been rewritten to change those records.

`protected-assets.json` records SHA256 for1,341 source/runtime/art/Juni files before G3-A. `preservation-and-native-proof.json` verifies those inputs remain identical. Original Gate1, Gate2.5 branch, old approval tag and main are preserved. New branch: `art-gate/gate3-full-world`.

The versioned production plan wraps, without edits, `assets-src/gate25/world-plan-v1.json` (4352×4864). North artwork registers at(640,650); Jeju at(3180,3200), scale1. The approved bridge/arrival collision proof registers at(2350,3100). Original expanded Jeju planning authority supersedes older stale reservation labels in frozen Slice C metadata. No ferry/train mechanics have been created.

`tools/gate3_assets.py` creates90 world and21 district chunks by exact512px crops. Edges use their true remaining dimensions. Approved native ground PNGs are copied at integer coordinates; no runtime scaling, palette conversion, recoloring, image generation or source resampling. All111 PNG/mask pairs have recorded SHA256. Reconstruction compares every chunk pixel/mask byte to the offline logical source, including borders. New engineering colors, rectangles, route strokes and labels are explicitly unfinished planning geometry, not new production art. A full logical image exists only during offline baking; the runtime never loads a4352×4864 texture.

Foreground uses original individual source PNGs, native sizes, support-root Y depths and existing alpha-softening policy. Foreground remains whole across chunk boundaries; the cache requests its full bounds/dependencies, never cuts a tree/object into ground tiles. No background reconstruction or new artwork was performed. The corrected district uses `district-refinement/west.png`, `district-refinement/center.png`, `district-planter-repair/east.png` and all19 original foreground objects, including the two corrected planters. It is an independent coordinate laboratory until world-registration review.

Original Juni atlas1202×1394,96×112 animation cells, anchor48/100 and motion source are unchanged; sprites are scale1. Shared joystick, session metrics, style and tour type remain original imports. `FoundationCamera.ts` adapts the approved ReviewCamera source from01f4f95 into manifest-supplied world stops and a0.5/1 detail zoom range. Overview uses a separately identified small planning proxy rather than loading the whole world. The Gate2.5 camera/runtime files are unchanged.

This foundation demonstrates preservation/rendering of approved buildings, not authorship of new southern architecture. Future G3-B must author distinct native-size buildings against Muneum/bakery craftsmanship, validate each in engine with Juni and collision/entrance evidence, and review localized connections before reuse. Duplicating/recoloring these buildings cannot satisfy G3-B.

Chunk images are a streaming terrain format, not a reusable tile vocabulary. Painted facades, perspective shadows, vegetation edges and ground ownership still require deliberate segmentation/repainting into modular assets if desired. A tilemap migration cannot infer usable tiles or colliders from a flattened plate. Texture/collision ownership must stay in one integer world coordinate system; whole foreground roots may span several chunks and must be retained once. Scene-to-engineering boundaries are unfinished visible edges, not solved production seams.

New materials/artwork for unfinished districts and coast/bridge transitions are G3-B work after this checkpoint review. No external generation session, paid API or unrecorded asset is required to reproduce G3-A.
