# V7 — Living Korea world reconstruction candidate

This is a real4352×5312 Phaser implementation with a new editable road/site graph, moved native landmark instances and matching physics. It is an internal art-QC candidate for the user's visual approval, not a public release or a declaration that the user accepted the artwork.

## What was reconstructed

The active V7 map does not import V6's southern ground, layered road repairs or final-lock surface finish. `tools/build_v7_world.py` starts from original image materials and one clean graph (`routes.json`, `courts.json`), exporting an editable native ground, masks, reusable32px tiles/atlas, separate native props and Forge Tiled data. Earlier source/entry/review versions remain unchanged for recovery. The previous northern repair mask identifies pixels to **erase** only at the authorized southern handoff; it is never imported as a V7 road layout.

The station is now the central destination: two garden approaches meet its south facade, with a balanced entrance forecourt, independent library/cafe frontage plots, actual timber rail crossing, platform stair and separate public gathering street. This replaces the previous crowded band of small buildings below the railway. Namsan has a real main entrance, complete curved tower loop, secondary woodland trail, observation court and native terrace stairs within its own railed/planted boundary. The amusement gateway is centered within a separate campus; one loop serves distinct Ferris/carousel courts, kiosk garden and discovery hall. Plants and seating occupy designed off-route pockets, not old-road concealment patches.

Terminal, waiting court, narrow boarding pier and boat align visibly. The former unexplained oversized sea deck is gone from the active scene; its approved lookout artwork is reused on **land** as a distinct coastal viewing destination. A short usable bridge crosses an actual engineered coastal inlet. Ferry travel remains the primary mainland–Jeju connection: a continuous land bridge would bypass the established ferry interaction and contradict the open-water separation. The visible narrow gangways, boarding interactions and reciprocal island landing provide the designed crossing sequence; no decorative bridge is presented as a blocked player route.

Jeju has a new curved arrival, volcanic-hill loop, village approach around its courtyard, learning garden, nature-centre stair and southern coastal circuit/viewpoint. Source paintings, Juni's144frames, north architecture, dialogues, lessons and landscape joystick are preserved. No new NPC/gameplay district, ride minigame or backend was created.
