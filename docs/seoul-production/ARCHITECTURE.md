R1–R4 are the official Korean Heritage Service/UNESCO sources recorded with URLs, access/hash evidence and limitations in [historical references](../urban-zoning/HISTORICAL_REFERENCES.md) and references.json: Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Korean seowon and Hahoe/Yangdong. Those are architectural/urban references; no photographs or existing game art were imported into the asset pipeline. The district is fictional and not a faithful reconstruction of any real town or period.

All approved masses, roads, original entrances, courtyards, palace boundary and future water/bridge coordinates remain unchanged in the frozen masterplan. Its old planning-status string is retained as provenance; the later user approval is recorded in docs/current/BUILD_LIVING_SEOUL.md. Runtime implements only the northern region. Main gateway now allows a controlled visit to the FIRST outer court; this limited interactive interpretation is recorded separately from the original closed-context plan. Inner palace and side service gates stay closed. Ordinary district streets are not fortified.

Landscape placement has explicit reasons in outdoor-bundle.json: academy orchard, workshop rear yard, destination planting, shade/rest pockets, entrance lanterns and service jars. Only tree trunks are solid; roofs/canopies use actual opaque-pixel Y-sort and Juni's existing visibility cue. Academy wings have separate sorting and collisions, keeping the court open. No building is a palette-swapped duplicate.
