# Tham khảo lịch sử đã đọc — quy hoạch tinh chỉnh

Khu phố là thiết kế hư cấu nguyên gốc. Các nguồn dưới đây hướng dẫn quan hệ cổng–sân–đường–nhà–cảnh quan, không cung cấp assets để chép hoặc một bản đồ lịch sử để tái dựng. Đã kiểm tra nội dung thực, không chỉ HTTP200; chi tiết đáp ứng/hash: [references.json](references.json).

## R1 — Korea Heritage Service / Royal Palaces and Tombs Center — Gyeongbokgung Palace and Its History

Nguồn: [https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E101010000.do?schGroupCode=gbg&schGroupCodeNm=Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E101010000.do?schGroupCode=gbg&schGroupCodeNm=Gyeongbokgung)

Primary Joseon palace, wide Yukjo Street before the main Gwanghwamun gate, and named gates in several directions.

Áp dụng nguyên gốc: A straight civic approach, large south gate and defined palace boundary with service gates.

Giới hạn: Not the real Gyeongbokgung plan, scale, current restoration or palace program.

## R2 — UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Changdeokgung Palace Complex

Nguồn: [https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/816/](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/816/)

Formal/residential court hierarchy within an architectural complex adapted to uneven terrain; terraces, pools and pavilions form part of the garden.

Áp dụng nguyên gốc: A legible inner gate/court relationship, secondary paths that follow landscape and an open pavilion framing the pond.

Giới hạn: No copied Secret Garden, palace footprint, three-court reconstruction or claimed topographic survey.

## R3 — UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies

Nguồn: [https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1498/](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1498/)

Teaching, scholar veneration and engagement with nature are reflected in spatial organization; pavilion buildings mediate between learning and landscape.

Áp dụng nguyên gốc: A quiet teaching court, separate study/library wings and a garden-facing rest place.

Giới hạn: Not a named historic academy or a claim that this fictional Hangul school is a historic seowon.

## R4 — UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Historic Villages of Korea: Hahoe and Yangdong

Nguồn: [https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324/](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324/)

Joseon-era villages relate houses, study halls and pavilions to forested slopes, river and fields; varied household groups participate in the settlement structure.

Áp dụng nguyên gốc: Different U/L/detached residential compounds, plots and alleys responding to courts, household service yards and water.

Giới hạn: Not a Bukchon/Hahoe/Yangdong map or a historical shop reconstruction. Bookshop/tea/craft uses are original fictional game programs adapted to this architectural grammar.
