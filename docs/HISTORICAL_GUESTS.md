# Historical guests — original educational interpretations

These fictional story projections visit a Korean-inspired social town. They lived in different periods; the game explicitly does not claim they lived together. Original pixel geometry shares the accepted player's anatomy/palette, with no copied modern portrait, banknote, game character or costume image. Costumes are historically informed stylizations, not museum reconstructions. Source: tools/guest_art.py. Korean/Vietnamese wording is authored for this prototype, still pending qualified educational review; no official endorsement.

Sejong: Hunminjeongeum was promulgated in1446 under his reign. Jang: court engineer/scientist/inventor, associated with astronomical instruments and water clocks; the prototype does not claim sole rain-gauge authorship or invent precise life dates. Saimdang: Joseon artist/calligrapher/poet, presented through her creative practice. Yi: Joseon admiral during the Imjin War; no exaggerated battle statistics, violent quest or modern mythology.
