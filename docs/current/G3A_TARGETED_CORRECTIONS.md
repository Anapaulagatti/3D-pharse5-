## TASK 2 — Isolate WebGL Performance Issues

The current software-rendered WebGL benchmark has a p95 frame interval of approximately 42ms, while isolated Canvas is approximately 17ms.

These results do not establish actual mobile GPU performance.

### Required work

Profile and compare:

- Frame update and rendering time.
- Ground and foreground draw calls.
- Texture uploads.
- Chunk admission, prefetch and eviction.
- Texture decoding and disposal.
- Collision-mask processing.
- Camera movement across chunk boundaries.
- Scenic zoom and Cinematic Tour transitions.
- CPU/GC pauses and other observed main-thread stalls.

Use controlled, repeatable, non-recorded benchmark sessions.

Separate cold loading, warm stationary rendering, movement across chunks and stress testing.

Compare WebGL and Canvas using equivalent scenes and conditions.

Optimize actual identified bottlenecks rather than blindly reducing artwork resolution.

Preserve the native pixel-art quality.

Do not present SwiftShader performance as certified physical-device performance.
