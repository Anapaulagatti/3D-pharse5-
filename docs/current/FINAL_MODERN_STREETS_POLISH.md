## 2. REBUILD THE MODERN ROAD LANGUAGE

The current southern roads look too much like irregular gray paving scattered between attractions.

Introduce a clear, modern urban circulation system:

- A recognizable primary boulevard or pedestrian spine.
- Secondary streets connecting individual attractions.
- Smaller walkways entering parks and leisure areas.
- Defined plazas in front of stations, shops and entertainment venues.
- Proper modern sidewalks, curbs and surface transitions.
- Distinct materials for roads, pedestrian areas, parks and waterfront promenades.
- Consistent street widths and understandable intersections.

Use contemporary Korean urban-design inspiration.

Modern streets should feel engineered and intentional, rather than organic medieval paths.

Avoid unnecessary diagonal roads, awkward turns, oversized empty paved areas or unrealistic intersections.

## 3. STRONG CONTEMPORARY MATERIALS

Differentiate the modern district from the heritage district using:

- Asphalt or appropriately paved urban streets.
- Light-gray concrete and engineered stone sidewalks.
- Modern interlocking paving patterns.
- Clean curb edges and drainage details.
- Contemporary metal railings and streetlights.
- Pedestrian crossings, road markings and tactile paving where functionally appropriate.
- Modern planters, benches, signage and wayfinding.
- Coordinated coastal promenade paving and viewing decks.

Do not overfill the streets with markings or decorations.

The main visual distinction should come from **materials, geometry and organized urban infrastructure**, not simply changing road colors.

## 4. ORGANIZE EACH MODERN DESTINATION

**Namsan:** Preserve organic hillside trails, but give the tower arrival area a contemporary observation plaza, clear pedestrian access, modern railings and appropriate paving.

**Bora Bloom:** Use clean, connected amusement-park walkways, a main entrance boulevard, attraction plazas and orderly queue areas. Visitors should intuitively understand how to move between rides.

**Modern commercial street:** Add a coherent sidewalk frontage, storefront access, small street furniture and a recognizable shopping-street atmosphere.

**Jeju:** Keep natural basalt paths and island character. Do NOT cover Jeju with asphalt just to make the world modern. Improve ferry-arrival paving only where appropriate.

Each area needs its own street treatment while sharing one consistent modern-world design language.
