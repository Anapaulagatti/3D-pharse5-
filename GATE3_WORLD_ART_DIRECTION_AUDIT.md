# G3-D world art-direction audit — rejected baseline, recovery review

**Current status:** the shared art system has conditional human approval for controlled ten-region propagation. See [current authority](docs/current/G3D_WORLD_RECOVERY_AUTHORIZATION.md) and [consolidated recovery review](docs/gate3-recovery-world/README.md). The audit below is the preserved diagnosis of the rejected baseline and the earlier three-pilot scope; its former propagation lock is superseded. It does not establish acceptance of the new world candidate.

Authority: [human recovery directive](docs/current/G3D_ART_RECOVERY_AUTHORIZATION.md). The G3-B/C engine milestone is retained; its **full-world art is rejected**. This audit is not production acceptance. The approved plan, old sources and checkpoints remain immutable. Only three representative recovery pilots are authorized before one unified-system approval.

## Evidence and method

Reviewed the [whole-world overview](docs/gate3-world/world-overview-review.jpg), then actual native Phaser captures for all ten planned regions. The overview establishes regional rhythm and projected envelopes; it cannot establish pixel quality. [Native audit boards](docs/gate3-recovery/audit-native-board-1.png), [board 2](docs/gate3-recovery/audit-native-board-2.png), [board 3](docs/gate3-recovery/audit-native-board-3.png) contain unscaled crops. [Capture registry](docs/gate3-recovery/audit-source-evidence.json) records source hashes, crop rectangles and original artifact commit. Full native images remain under `docs/gate3-world/` (1280×900, zoom1). No browser-scaled image is substituted for native evidence.

The canonical standard is the [Art Direction Bible](KOREAN_ARENA_ART_DIRECTION_BIBLE.md), derived from approved historical A, Muneum B, original Bakery, accepted station/restored floor, Jeju C and seamless district. Old giant tiles/new landmarks are defect evidence, never quality references.

## Shared cause and correction ownership

`tools/g3bc_build_world.py` stamps samples from `assets-src/gate3/full-world/materials-host.png` with periods 232–248px. Those samples contain already-large slabs and individual flower groups, creating coarse detail, repeated objects and hard ownership rectangles. Native rendering can be technically exact while showing poor native art. The source crops are not to be sharpened or resized. Replace the authored material language and region composition, with explicit masks and dependency-based chunk regeneration.

`production-assets/*` contains eight native extracted landmarks. Native-size extraction did not establish believable door proportions or stylistic consistency. `trees-native.png` generated trees roughly300×380px, much larger than the approved189×196px exemplar; repeated bright seasonal specimens dominate paths and cast inconsistent contacts. A new system must use full silhouettes, calm regional rhythm and explicit root/collision ownership. Do not hide defective joins with more trees.
