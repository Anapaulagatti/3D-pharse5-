# Living Pixel Seoul — unified native pixel bible v4

One warm, original Korean social-town kit. Previous minimalist/painterly A/B/C are rejected. User reference images inform readability, road hierarchy and coherent sprite density only; no copied assets, layouts, UI, brands or NFTs.

- Projection: top-down three-quarter, vertical walls and visible roof planes. Screen y is ground depth. Sun upper-left; hard pixel highlights, two shadow steps, contact shadow southeast.
- Grid: native32px square tiles; no downscaled paintings. Every visible art pixel is authored on the same1px grid. Buildings assembled from repeatable32px wood/window/roof bays, approximately128–192px wide and112–144px high. Avatar32×48, visible40px: doors32px wide/40px high, tree64×96. Avoid giant showcase illustrations.
- Camera: integer2× gameplay (visible player80–90CSSpx),1×overview,3×inspection. Round positions and native canvas dimensions. Nearest-neighbour, no smoothing, pixelated CSS, DPR1/2 tested. Canvas never stretched to fit a fractional image scale.
- Palette: one shared ramp dictionary in tools/export_unified.py. Warm ink, indigo giwa roof4steps, amber timber4steps, cream plaster3steps, celadon/coral accents, foliage4steps, gray-green stone4steps. No per-object arbitrary gradients, photographic textures or antialiasing. Transparent or opaque alpha only.
- Buildings: modular timber wall/window/door bays, recessed frames, raised stone foundation/steps, thick eaves, patterned two-plane tile roof and side wall. Courier station, café, hanok and academy share the kit with distinct signs/porches/awnings. Base footprints collide; roof art sorts by ground footpoint.
- Ground: varied stone/brick/path tiles, corner/straight curbs and grass seams; flower beds, gardens, seats, posts and stalls support occupied town edges. Keep the center and route junctions free for social gatherings.
- Trees:64×96 grouped canopy, distinct dark underclusters, short highlights, readable branching trunk and root contact. Pine/plum/broadleaf use identical density/ramp logic. Object shadows are original native sprites at ground depth.
- Layers: Ground(-100), Edging(-90), contact shadows(-50), object sprites by integer footpoint y; player feet and NPC feet sort likewise. Transparent roof overhangs do not enlarge collisions. Label demo NPCs; accessible Korean reading lives in HTML dialogue.
- Avatar:32×48 anchor(16,47), body/skin base + outfit + hair + optional satchel, all four directions/all animation frames registered. Two hairstyles (wavy crop/tied bob), two constructed outfits (courier vest/academy jacket). Clothes, hands and hair move with the same motion key. Base footsteps retain collision16×8 at feet.
- Animation: idle4frames with natural120ms blink and gentle breath; walk8 distinct phases at10fps; run8 separately drawn airborne/lean/arm-stride phases at13fps (never remapped walk). Wave6, celebration8, social pose4, all four directions. Emotes have bounded duration and return to idle. OS reduced motion freezes ambient/idle motion, retains voluntary player movement/emotes. Pause/visibility freezes simulation.
- Atlas: per layer512×1024RGBA,2px gutters,152native frames/layer, at most64shared colors per avatar layer. Immutable frame names/action-direction-index; individual editable PNGs and metadata retained. Shared asset directory under public-unified only; rejected sources excluded from runtime.
- Gate2 budget: one32×24tile map, modular building kit, three tree silhouettes, readable gathering plaza and landmark stations, no backend/quest/ad SDK. Native tiled/source equality, frame registration/run difference/collision reachability checked. Build and actual Phaser images/video required. Cloud samples are not physical-device FPS claims.

Subjective beauty and cohesion require the user's review. STOP before Gate3 or publication.
